About me

Hi, I'm Andrea. I like to automate things.

I'm a young engineer into automation, open source and technology in general. FlowingBot is my lab: the place where I build my projects, test new tools and run my home lab.

How a signal moves through the lab

Sense. Transmit. Orchestrate. Observe.

Most of my projects follow the same four-stage path. It's the actual pipeline behind EdgeHub.

01 / SENSE

Edge instrumentation

Go and C++ agents read CPU, thermal, and vibration data directly off Raspberry Pi, ESP32, and bare-metal hosts, instead of polling a dashboard that's already stale.

02 / TRANSMIT

Reliable delivery

MQTT and stateless JWT pipelines move telemetry off the device. Tokens rotate over the air, so a fleet doesn't quietly drop offline.

03 / ORCHESTRATE

Cloud-native control

Kubernetes, GitOps, and zero-trust proxies turn raw ingestion into a system that scales, and that someone other than me can operate safely.

04 / OBSERVE

Decisions, not charts

Prometheus, Loki, and Grafana close the loop. The point isn't a pretty graph, it's catching the failure before it happens.

How I work

What I believe.

Workflows & Agents

Core operations should be boring, deterministic workflows that just work. AI agents come in where the context is fuzzy and the edge cases pile up.

IIoT is not optional

Sensors and physical machines are the foundation of everything I build, not an afterthought. If the data is wrong at the source, nothing downstream can fix it.

Built to be operated

I love open source because it pushes good habits: clear docs, simple setups, things someone else can actually run. Most of what I build is meant to be operated by someone who isn't me.
